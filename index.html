About

We sweat the details so you can win the room.

Ground Game is a political consulting firm based in Toronto. We work with candidates, nomination contestants, and advocacy campaigns who need a partner that understands both the strategy and the grind of field work.

We’re hands-on. We build the plan, stand up the tools, train the volunteers, and stay in the trenches with you through election day. No bloated retainers, no cookie-cutter playbooks — just the operation your campaign needs.
